About Our School

Excellence in Education Since 1995

📜

Our History

Kongunga High School was established in 1995 with a vision to provide quality education that nurtures young minds. What started with just 50 students has now grown into a premier institution with over 2000 students and 100+ dedicated teachers.

Learn More
🎯

Our Vision

To be a center of educational excellence that fosters creativity, critical thinking, and character development in every student, preparing them to become responsible global citizens.

Learn More
🚀

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that help them achieve academic excellence while becoming responsible, compassionate, and innovative leaders in their communities.

Learn More

📍 Our Location

Kongunga High School is strategically located in the heart of Kachumbala, Bukedea District, making it easily accessible from Mbale City and surrounding areas.

About the PTA

The Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) is a vital link between parents, teachers, and school administration. The PTA Board works collaboratively to enhance the educational experience, support school activities, and foster a strong school community.

PTA Functions and Responsibilities

  • Organizing fundraising activities for school projects
  • Facilitating communication between parents and school administration
  • Supporting school events and celebrations
  • Providing feedback on school policies and parent concerns
  • Mobilizing parent volunteers for school activities
  • Supporting student welfare and scholarship programs

PTA Meetings Schedule

General PTA Meetings: Once per term (February, June, September)
Executive Committee Meetings: Monthly on the last Saturday
Location: School Hall or via Zoom (links sent via parent WhatsApp groups)

Get Involved

All parents are automatically members of the PTA. You can get involved by:

  • Attending PTA meetings
  • Volunteering for school events
  • Contributing to fundraising initiatives
  • Serving on a sub-committee
  • Providing professional expertise (medical, legal, construction, etc.)

Student Testimonials

Hear from our students about their experiences at Kongunga High School

TZ

JULIUS ODEKE

Coding Club - Form 4

My experience in Kongunga High School is nourishing and wonderful. In my class, I have good friends who cooperate and help me when I am in need. This school is also like an international school with loads of international students from countries like South Sudan, India, Congo, and South Africa. In addition to that, this school has awesome teachers who are professionals in teaching. They teach very well that students understand each and every topic. All hard work brings profit but mere talk leads to poverty.

SA

NABALAYO CATHERINE

Debate Club - Form 4

My experience in Kongunga High School dates back to form one when I joined. I learnt so many different things that have actually helped me a lot of times. The teaching staff at Kongunga is exceptional when it comes to their subjects and they always make sure to help us students pass our papers highly. I don't regret joining Kongunga because I have made a lot of friends and built many positive relationships with them. I hope to see more students joining this wonderful institution.

RR

THOMAS OPIO

Red Cross Club - Form 6

Kongunga High School has very well disciplined students who come together when one is in need and they all promote cooperation and team work. Secondly, the school has almost all nationalities of students and these include the Indians, Kenyans, South Sudanese, Congolese etc. In addition to that, the school has a very green and nice environment and the teachers are caring, loving, cooperative and they are always available when we are in need. I have grown both academically and spiritually during my time here.

JO

JOHN OKELLO

Science Club - Form 5

The science department at Kongunga High School is amazing! The laboratories are well equipped and the teachers are always ready to help us understand complex concepts. I have developed a strong passion for Physics and Chemistry. The school also offers great career guidance that helped me choose my future path in Medicine. I am grateful for the opportunities I have received. Kongunga has truly shaped my future.

GN

MICHEAL OLUPOT

Netball Club - Form 4

I joined Kongunga High School in Form 3 and it was the best decision my parents ever made. The sports facilities are top notch and I have represented the school in several netball competitions. The teachers balance academics and co-curricular activities perfectly. I am proud to be a Kongunga student. The friendships I have made here will last a lifetime and the skills I have learned are invaluable.

PW

PETER WASSWA

ICT Club - Form 3

The ICT department at Kongunga is outstanding. I have learned coding, web design, and even robotics. The school provides modern computers and internet access. I can confidently say that Kongunga has prepared me for the digital future. The teachers are patient and always willing to go the extra mile to ensure we understand every concept. I am now able to create websites and simple applications.

MA

MARY ACHIENG

Literary Club - Form 5

The library at Kongunga High School is my favorite place. With over 10,000 books and digital resources, I have been able to research and learn beyond the classroom. The reading culture here is amazing. The school also organizes career fairs that have opened my eyes to many opportunities. I am excited about my future because of Kongunga. This school has given me the tools I need to succeed in life.

Admissions 2026-27

Join the Kongunga Family Today!

📅 Admission Open for Academic Year 2026-27

Limited seats available. Apply now for both O-Level & A-Level.

🎯 Eligibility Criteria
  • O-Level (S.1 - S.4): Completion of Primary 7 with PLE results
  • A-Level (S.5 - S.6): UCE certificate with at least 8 passes
  • Transfer Students: Accepted based on previous academic performance
  • Age Requirement (S.1): 12-14 years by March 31, 2026
  • Age Requirement (S.5): 16-18 years by March 31, 2026
📋 Required Documents
  • Birth Certificate (original + copy)
  • Previous class report card (last 2 terms)
  • Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photos (4 copies)
  • Parent/Guardian National ID copy
  • Address proof (LC letter or utility bill)
  • Immunization/Health records
  • PLE/UCE Result Slip (scanned copy - upload in the form)

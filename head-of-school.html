Message from the HeadTeacher

"Dear Parents, Students, and Well-wishers,

It is my honor and privilege to lead Kongunga High School, an institution that has been transforming young lives for three decades. Our commitment to academic excellence, character formation, and holistic development remains unwavering.

I believe that every child is born with unique gifts and talents. Our role as educators is to discover, nurture, and polish those gifts until they shine brightly. At Kongunga, we don't just prepare students for examinations - we prepare them for life.

Our dedicated team of teachers works tirelessly to create a supportive environment where students feel safe, valued, and challenged to reach their full potential. We emphasize discipline, hard work, and integrity as the foundation for success.

I invite you to visit our campus and experience the Kongunga difference. Together, let us build a brighter future for your children and our nation."

With warm regards,
Dr. Oitabo Stephene

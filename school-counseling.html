School Counseling Services

Supporting student wellbeing and success

Guidance and Counseling Program

Our comprehensive counseling program addresses the academic, career, and personal/social development of all students.

Counseling Services Offered

Academic Counseling

Subject selection, study skills, exam preparation, and time management strategies.

Career Counseling

Career exploration, university applications, scholarship guidance, and job placement support.

Personal Counseling

Confidential support for emotional challenges, peer pressure, self-esteem, and family issues.

Crisis Intervention

Immediate support for students facing emergencies or critical situations.

Counseling Team

Our counseling department consists of certified guidance counselors, a school psychologist, and trained peer counselors who work together to support student wellbeing.

How to Access Counseling

  • Self-referral: Students can walk in during break times
  • Teacher referral: Teachers can refer students needing support
  • Parent referral: Parents can request counseling for their children
  • Peer referral: Friends can recommend counseling services

Counseling Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Nutrition and Dining

Healthy meals for growing minds

School Meals Program

At Kongunga High School, we provide nutritious meals to all boarding students and day scholars who opt for the meal plan. Our kitchen follows strict hygiene standards and offers balanced meals.

Special Diets

Students with allergies, medical conditions, or religious dietary requirements can request special meals. Contact the Kitchen Manager at least one week in advance.

Dining Hall Information

  • Capacity: 500 students per sitting
  • Meal Times: Breakfast 7:00-7:45 AM, Lunch 1:00-2:00 PM, Supper 6:00-7:00 PM
  • Dress Code: Students must be in proper school attire during meals

Meal Fees (Per Term)

  • Full Boarding Students: Included in boarding fees
  • Day Scholars (Lunch only): UGX 150,000
  • Day Scholars (All meals): UGX 300,000

Our Academic Programs

At Kongunga High School, we are committed to providing a holistic education that nurtures academic excellence, character development, and lifelong learning. Our academic programs are designed to prepare students for success in higher education and beyond.

📚

Academic Overview

Kongunga High School offers a comprehensive and balanced curriculum that caters to the diverse needs of our students. We follow the Ugandan national curriculum and prepare students for both O-Level and A-Level examinations.

Learn More
🎯

Academic Vision

Our vision is to be a center of academic excellence that produces well-rounded, critical thinkers who are equipped to compete globally while remaining grounded in Ugandan values and culture.

Learn More
📖

Academic Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to provide a balanced education that includes sciences, humanities, arts, and vocational subjects. We emphasize practical skills, critical thinking, and character development.

Learn More
📚

O-Level (S1 - S4)

Foundation years focusing on core subjects and character development. Students sit for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examinations.

  • Mathematics
  • English Language
  • Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Humanities (History, Geography, CRE)
  • Computer Science
🎓

A-Level (S5 - S6)

Advanced studies preparing students for university. Students sit for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) examinations.

  • Science Combinations
  • Arts Combinations
  • Business Combinations
  • ICT and Computer Science

Curriculum Highlights

STEM Education

Strong focus on Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics with modern laboratories.

Arts & Humanities

Rich programs in Literature, History, Economics, and Creative Arts.

ICT Integration

Computer labs, smart classrooms, and digital learning resources.

Career Guidance

Comprehensive counseling and career pathway planning.

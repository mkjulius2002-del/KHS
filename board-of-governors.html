Board of Governors

Providing strategic leadership and oversight

About the Board

The Board of Governors is the highest governing body of Kongunga High School, responsible for strategic direction, policy approval, and overall accountability. The board consists of distinguished individuals from education, business, and community leadership.

Board Responsibilities

  • Setting the strategic direction and vision of the school
  • Approving annual budgets and major expenditures
  • Appointing and evaluating the HeadTeacher
  • Ensuring compliance with Ministry of Education regulations
  • Overseeing infrastructure development
  • Reviewing and approving school policies
  • Ensuring financial accountability and transparency

Board Meetings

The Board of Governors meets quarterly (March, June, September, December). Special meetings may be called as needed. Parents and staff may submit matters for board consideration through the HeadTeacher's office.

Board Committees

  • Finance & Audit Committee: Oversees financial management
  • Academic Committee: Reviews academic performance and curriculum
  • Infrastructure Committee: Plans and monitors development projects
  • Human Resources Committee: Handles staff welfare and employment matters
  • Discipline Committee: Addresses serious student discipline issues

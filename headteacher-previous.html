Previous Headteachers

Honoring the leaders who shaped Kongunga High School

A Legacy of Leadership

Since its founding in 1995, Kongunga High School has been blessed with visionary leaders who have guided the school to excellence. Each Headteacher has left an indelible mark on our institution.

DS

Dr. Oitabo Stephene

Current Headteacher (2020 - Present)
Dr. Oitabo became Headteacher in 2020 and has since led the school through digital transformation, infrastructure expansion, and achieving record-breaking UNEB results. Under his leadership, enrollment has grown to over 2,000 students.
Current Headteacher
MN

Mrs. Grace Nambi

Headteacher (2010 - 2020)
Mrs. Nambi served for 10 years, during which she oversaw the construction of the science block, computer laboratory, and the expansion of the library. She introduced the school's first ICT curriculum and established the parent-teacher partnership program.
Former Headteacher
MM

Mr. John Mukasa

Headteacher (2000 - 2010)
Mr. Mukasa led the school during its relocation to the current campus. He oversaw the construction of the main administration block, dormitories, and dining hall. Under his leadership, the school achieved its first 100% UNEB pass rate in 2005.
Former Headteacher

Honoring Our Leaders

Each Headteacher has been honored with a named facility at the school:

  • Peter Ochieng Hall - The main assembly hall
  • John Mukasa Science Block
  • Grace Nambi Computer Laboratory
  • Dr. Oitabo Stephene Library (under construction)

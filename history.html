30 Years of Excellence

A journey of transforming lives since 1995

Our Historic Journey

Kongunga High School has been a beacon of quality education in Kachumbala, Bukedea for three decades. From humble beginnings to becoming one of the most respected schools in the region, our journey is one of dedication, perseverance, and excellence.

📅 1995 - The Beginning

Kongunga High School was founded by a group of visionary educators led by the late Mr. James Kongunga. The school started with just 45 students and 7 teachers in a small rented building in Kachumbala town.

📅 1998 - First UNEB Registration

The school registered with Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) and presented its first candidates for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examinations. All 30 candidates passed in Division One and Two.

📅 2002 - Relocation to Current Campus

Kongunga High School moved to its current 10-acre campus, with new classrooms, a library, and a science laboratory. Student enrollment grew to 500.

📅 2005 - Introduction of A-Level

The school introduced Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) program, offering Science, Arts, and Business combinations.

📅 2010 - First A-Level Graduates

The first cohort of A-Level students graduated, with 80% qualifying for university admission, including Makerere University and Kyambogo University.

📅 2015 - Infrastructure Expansion

New computer laboratory, modern science labs, and a sports complex were completed, making Kongunga a model school in Bukedea District.

📅 2020 - Digital Transformation

The school adopted a blended learning model with smart classrooms, online resources, and a student information system.

📅 2025 - 30th Anniversary & Beyond

Kongunga High School celebrates 30 years of excellence with over 5,000 alumni worldwide. The school continues to innovate and expand, with plans for a new STEM center and boarding facilities.

Our Legacy

Today, Kongunga High School stands proud as a center of academic excellence with over 2,000 students, 100+ staff members, and a reputation for producing outstanding graduates who serve as doctors, engineers, lawyers, teachers, entrepreneurs, and leaders in Uganda and beyond.

School Library

A world of knowledge at your fingertips

Library Overview

Kongunga High School Library is a modern, well-stocked resource center designed to support teaching, learning, and research. With over 10,000 books, digital resources, and comfortable study spaces, our library is the heart of academic life at Kongunga.

Library Collections

📚 General Collection

Over 8,000 books covering all subjects in the curriculum, reference books, encyclopedias, and dictionaries.

📖 Textbook Collection

Multiple copies of UNEB recommended textbooks for all subjects and grade levels.

📰 Periodicals & Newspapers

Daily newspapers (Daily Monitor, New Vision), educational magazines, and academic journals.

💻 Digital Library

10 computers with internet access, e-books, online databases, and educational software.

📚 Fiction & Literature

African literature, international classics, young adult fiction, and poetry collections.

Library Services

  • Borrowing: Students may borrow up to 3 books for 2 weeks
  • Reference Service: Librarian assistance for research projects
  • Reading Programs: Weekly reading hours and book clubs
  • Study Spaces: Silent study area and group discussion rooms
  • Photocopying: Available at a small fee
  • Internet Access: Free Wi-Fi for research purposes

Library Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed
Examination Period: Extended hours until 7:00 PM

Library Rules

  • Silence must be observed at all times
  • No food or drinks allowed
  • Books must be returned on time (late fee: UGX 1,000 per day)
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for
  • Mobile phones must be switched off or on silent mode
  • Library cards must be presented for borrowing

Special Collections

Past Papers Archive

Complete collection of UNEB past papers (2000-2025) with marking guides for revision.

Teacher Resource Center

Professional development materials, teaching guides, and curriculum resources for staff.

Library Staff

  • Head Librarian: Mrs. Sarah Nambi - MLIS, Makerere University
  • Assistant Librarian: Mr. James Okello - Diploma in Library & Information Science
  • Library Assistant: Miss Grace Atim

📢 Announcement

The library now offers e-book access through our digital portal. Students can read over 1,000 e-books from any device with internet connection. See the librarian for login credentials.

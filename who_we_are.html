Who We Are

Discover the story behind Kongunga High School

Our Story

Kongunga High School was established in 1995 with a vision to transform education in Kachumbala, Bukedea District. What began as a small school with just 50 students has grown into one of the region's most respected educational institutions.

Today, we serve over 2,000 students with a team of 100+ dedicated teachers and staff members. Our commitment to academic excellence, character development, and holistic education has made us a preferred choice for families across Uganda.

We are affiliated with the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) and consistently achieve 100% pass rates in both O-Level and A-Level examinations.

Our Values

Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in everything we do.

Integrity: We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical behavior.

Innovation: We embrace modern teaching methods and technology.

Community: We foster a supportive and inclusive school family.

Employment Opportunities

Join our dedicated team of educators

Work at Kongunga High School

Kongunga High School is always looking for passionate, qualified, and dedicated individuals to join our team. We offer a supportive work environment, professional development opportunities, and competitive compensation.

🏃‍♂️ Sports Coach

Requirements: Diploma in Physical Education, coaching certification.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit the following documents to the HeadTeacher's office or email to hr@kongungahighschool.edu:

  • Application letter
  • Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Certified copies of academic certificates
  • Teaching license (for teaching positions)
  • National ID copy
  • Three reference letters (including one from previous employer)

Application Deadline

All applications must be received by the respective deadlines mentioned above. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews.

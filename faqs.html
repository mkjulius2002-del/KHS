Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Kongunga High School

When is the admission deadline? +

Admissions for the 2026-27 academic year are currently open. We encourage early applications as seats are limited. The deadline will be announced on our website and social media pages.

What are the school fees? +

Our fees vary depending on the level (O-Level or A-Level) and whether the student is a boarder or day scholar. Please visit our Fees Structure page for detailed information.

Is there a scholarship program? +

Yes, we offer merit-based and need-based scholarships for outstanding students. Please contact our admissions office for more information about available scholarships and the application process.

What extracurricular activities are available? +

We offer a wide range of clubs and activities including Science Club, ICT Club, Debate Club, Football, Netball, Basketball, Drama, Music, and many more. Students are encouraged to participate in at least one activity.

How can I schedule a school visit? +

School tours are available Monday through Friday. Please contact our admissions office at +256 786706774 or email info@kongungahighschool.edu to schedule your visit.

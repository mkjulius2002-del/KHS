Health Services

Caring for student wellness

School Health Center

Our on-campus health center provides basic medical care for students and staff. A qualified nurse is available during school hours, and a doctor visits twice weekly.

Health Center Hours

Monday - Friday: 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM
Saturday: 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Sunday: Closed (Emergency contact available)

Services Offered

  • First aid and emergency care
  • Routine health check-ups
  • Vaccination programs
  • Health education and awareness campaigns
  • Referral to nearby hospitals for serious cases

Health Insurance

All students are required to have valid health insurance. The school can recommend affordable insurance providers for parents.

Health Awareness Programs

Sexual Health Education

Age-appropriate education on reproductive health, HIV/AIDS prevention, and healthy relationships.

Mental Health Support

Counseling services for stress, anxiety, depression, and other mental health concerns.

Nutrition and Wellness

Workshops on healthy eating, physical activity, and lifestyle choices.

Emergency Procedures

In case of medical emergency, the school follows a clear protocol:

  1. Immediate first aid at health center
  2. Parent/guardian notification
  3. Transport to Bukedea General Hospital or Mbale Regional Referral Hospital
  4. Follow-up care coordination

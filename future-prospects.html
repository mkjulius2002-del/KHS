Future Prospects for Students

Preparing students for successful futures

Career Pathways

At Kongunga High School, we prepare students not just for exams, but for life. Our comprehensive career guidance program helps students identify their strengths and explore various career options.

University Placement

  • Local Universities: Makerere University, Kyambogo University, MUBS, Uganda Christian University, and more
  • Regional Universities: University of Nairobi (Kenya), University of Dar es Salaam (Tanzania)
  • International Universities: UK, USA, Canada, and Australia through our partnership programs

Career Guidance Services

Career Counseling

One-on-one counseling sessions to help students choose the right subject combinations and career paths.

Career Fairs

Annual career fairs with professionals from various fields sharing insights.

Alumni Mentorship

Our successful alumni mentor current students on career choices and opportunities.

Scholarship Opportunities

We guide students in applying for national and international scholarships including Government of Uganda scholarships, Commonwealth scholarships, Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, and university-specific awards.

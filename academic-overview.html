Academic Overview

A comprehensive approach to education

Our Educational Philosophy

At Kongunga High School, we believe education extends beyond textbooks and examinations. Our academic program is designed to develop critical thinking, creativity, and character in every student.

Key Academic Features

  • Student-Centered Learning: Active participation and inquiry-based approaches
  • Continuous Assessment: Regular tests, assignments, and projects
  • Remedial Programs: Extra support for struggling students
  • Enrichment Activities: Advanced learning for high achievers
  • Career Guidance: Professional counseling for future pathways

Academic Calendar

The academic year runs from February to November, with three terms. Each term includes continuous assessment, mid-term exams, and end-of-term examinations.

Term Dates 2026

Term 1: February 5 - April 30
Term 2: May 15 - August 15
Term 3: September 5 - November 30

Spiritual Life

Nurturing faith and moral values

Faith Formation

At Kongunga High School, we recognize the importance of spiritual development alongside academic excellence. We provide opportunities for students to grow in their faith and moral values.

Christian Religious Education (CRE)

CRE is a core subject that teaches biblical principles, moral values, and ethical decision-making. Students learn to apply these teachings in their daily lives.

Worship Services

School Chapel

Every Wednesday morning from 8:00 AM - 8:30 AM. All students and staff are required to attend.

Sunday Service

Voluntary attendance at local churches near the school campus.

Religious Clubs and Fellowships

  • Christian Fellowship: Meets every Friday from 4:00 PM - 5:30 PM
  • Born Again Fellowship: Meets every Saturday from 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  • Catholic Students Association: Meets every Tuesday from 3:30 PM - 5:00 PM
  • Muslim Students Association: Friday prayers and Islamic studies

Moral Education

Weekly moral education classes focus on character development, ethics, and responsible citizenship. Topics include honesty, integrity, respect, and social responsibility.

Chapel Committee

Student-led committee that organizes spiritual activities, prepares worship services, and leads praise and worship.

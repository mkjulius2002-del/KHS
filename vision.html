Our Vision for the Future

Building tomorrow's leaders today

Our 10-Year Strategic Vision (2026-2036)

As we celebrate 30 years of excellence, we look forward to the next decade with ambitious goals for growth and innovation.

Key Strategic Pillars

📚 Academic Excellence

Maintain a 100% pass rate in UNEB examinations with 85% in Division One. Introduce specialized STEM and ICT programs.

🏗️ Infrastructure Development

Construct a modern science complex, boarding facilities for 500 students, and a digital resource center.

💻 Digital Transformation

Implement fully integrated e-learning platforms, smart classrooms, and a paperless administration system.

🌍 Global Partnerships

Establish exchange programs with international schools and partnerships with universities abroad.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Development

Institute continuous professional development programs and incentives to attract and retain top talent.

Facilities Expansion Plan

  • 2026-2027: Completion of girls' boarding wing (200 capacity)
  • 2027-2028: New STEM laboratory complex
  • 2028-2029: Olympic-size swimming pool and indoor sports arena
  • 2029-2030: Boys' boarding wing and dining hall expansion
  • 2030-2032: International standard auditorium and arts center

Enrollment Goals

  • Current: 2,000 students
  • By 2028: 2,500 students
  • By 2032: 3,000 students
  • By 2036: 3,500 students

Our Promise

Through all our growth and development, we remain committed to our core values of excellence, integrity, innovation, and compassion. Every decision we make will continue to prioritize the wellbeing and success of our students.

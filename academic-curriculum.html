🎯 O-Level Program Overview

The O-Level program (S1-S4) builds a strong academic foundation across multiple disciplines. Students study a broad range of subjects that develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and analytical skills. The program culminates in the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examination.

🎓 A-Level Program Overview

The A-Level program (S5-S6) offers specialized study in three principal subjects. Students choose combinations based on their career aspirations and academic strengths. The program prepares students for university education and professional careers, culminating in the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE).

📈 Grading Scale (UCE/UACE)

  • Division One (D1/D2): Aggregate 4-12 - Excellent
  • Division Two (C3/C4/C5/C6): Aggregate 13-24 - Good
  • Division Three (P7/P8): Aggregate 25-36 - Satisfactory
  • Division Four (S9/F9): Aggregate 37-48 - Pass

School Syllabus

Comprehensive curriculum coverage

O-Level Syllabus (S.1 - S.4)

Core Subjects (All Students)

  • Mathematics - Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics
  • English Language - Grammar, Composition, Literature
  • Physics - Mechanics, Electricity, Magnetism, Heat, Light
  • Chemistry - Atomic structure, Chemical reactions, Organic chemistry
  • Biology - Cell biology, Human anatomy, Plant biology, Ecology
  • Christian Religious Education (CRE) - Biblical teachings, Ethics, Values

Humanities Subjects

  • History - East African history, World history, Colonialism
  • Geography - Physical geography, Human geography, Map reading
  • Commerce - Trade, Business operations, Entrepreneurship

Practical Subjects

  • Computer Science - Basic computing, Programming, ICT skills
  • Agriculture - Crop farming, Animal husbandry, Farm management
  • Fine Art - Drawing, Painting, Design, Art history

A-Level Syllabus (S.5 - S.6)

Science Combinations

PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)

Career pathways: Engineering, Architecture, Computer Science, Physics, Chemistry

PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)

Career pathways: Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Nursing, Veterinary Medicine, Biochemistry

MEG (Mathematics, Economics, Geography)

Career pathways: Economics, Statistics, Urban Planning, Environmental Science

Arts Combinations

HEG (History, Economics, Geography)

Career pathways: Law, Public Administration, Economics, Journalism, Teaching

HEC (History, Economics, CRE)

Career pathways: Law, Theology, Social Work, Administration, Politics

LIT (Literature, Economics, Geography)

Career pathways: Journalism, Creative Writing, Media, Publishing, Marketing

Business Combinations

Commerce, Economics, Accounting

Career pathways: Accounting, Banking, Finance, Business Management, Entrepreneurship

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When does the admissions process begin?

▼

Admissions for the 2026-27 academic year opened on January 15, 2026. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis until all seats are filled.

Do you offer scholarships?

▼

Yes, we offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Please inquire with the Admissions Office for more details.

Is boarding available?

▼

Yes, we offer both day and boarding options. Boarding facilities are available for students in S1-S6.

What are the school fees?

▼

Fee structures vary by level (O-Level vs A-Level) and boarding status. Please visit our Fees Structure page for detailed information.

How can I schedule a campus tour?

▼

You can schedule a campus tour by contacting our Admissions Office via phone +256 786706774 or email admissions@kongungahighschool.edu

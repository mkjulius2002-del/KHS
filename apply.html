APPLICATION PROCESS

Take the first step toward an exceptional education

1

Submitting required documents

After the PLE or UNEB results are verified, the applicant must duly fill the school's admission form including all the required bio data of the student and parent/guardian. The applicant may access the admission form from the Director of Studies (D.O.S) office at Kongunga High School or fill the provided online admission form. Please note that a fee of UGX 10,000 shall be charged when picking the admission letter

2

Paying application fees

The school fees must be paid in full to the school account in Centenary Bank, or United Bank for Africa, before you come to school. Please pick the Bank slip from the school Bursar

3

After completing the online application.

Our admissions team will verify your submitted academic results and documents. And after verification, Within 24 hours of submission, you will receive a confirmation email with your student number.
